Nostalgia

I feel nostalgia, it feels like things are wrong and I am not in my right place, and I want to go back to the place of unfamiliarity - even if it was the place I left behind because I was worse off.

This will go away by degrees, I’m sure.

I will find a new home.

Hopefully one with you in it.
